PROBLEM STATEMENT
Product: Groww — Mutual Fund Facts-Only FAQ Assistant
=====================================================

BACKGROUND
Retail users struggle to find quick, factual answers about mutual fund schemes.
Details like expense ratio, exit load, minimum SIP amount, ELSS lock-in period,
riskometer level, benchmark index, and how to download statement documents are
scattered across factsheets, KIM/SID documents, scheme FAQs, and regulator pages.
Support and content teams at investment platforms answer the same repetitive
questions again and again.

WHY GROWW
Groww was selected as the product for this project (and for all upcoming
milestones). Groww operates its own AMC (Groww Mutual Fund), so the FAQ
corpus can be scoped to one AMC with 3-5 schemes under it.

WHO IT HELPS
- Retail users comparing schemes and trying to understand scheme facts.
- Support/content teams who need consistent, cited, facts-only answers.

WHAT THE ASSISTANT MUST DO
- Answer factual queries only, e.g.: expense ratio, ELSS lock-in, minimum SIP,
  exit load, riskometer/benchmark, how to download capital-gains statement.
- Show ONE clear citation link (official public page) in every answer.
- Politely refuse opinionated/portfolio questions ("Should I buy/sell?")
  with a facts-only message plus a relevant educational link.
- Provide a tiny UI: welcome line, 3 example questions, and the note
  "Facts-only. No investment advice."

SCOPE OF THE CORPUS
- One AMC: Groww Mutual Fund.
- 3-5 schemes under it (e.g., one large-cap, one flexi-cap, one ELSS).
- 15-25 public pages from AMC/SEBI/AMFI (factsheets, KIM/SID, scheme FAQs,
  fee/charges pages, riskometer/benchmark notes, statement/tax-doc guides).

KEY CONSTRAINTS
1. Public sources only (AMC/SEBI/AMFI). No third-party blogs.
2. Facts only — no investment advice: no buy/sell/hold recommendations.
3. No performance claims — never compute or compare returns; link to the
   official factsheet instead.
4. No PII — never accept or store PAN, Aadhaar, account numbers, OTPs,
   emails, or phone numbers.
5. Clarity — answers are at most 3 sentences, and include
   "Last updated from sources: <date>".

SUCCESS CRITERIA
- A working prototype (app or notebook) that answers the factual question
  types above with an accurate official citation every time.
- A source list (CSV/MD) of the 15-25 URLs used.
- README with setup steps, declared scope (AMC + schemes), and known limits.
- Sample Q&A file (5-10 queries with answers + links).
- The disclaimer snippet used in the UI.

SKILLS THIS EXERCISE TESTS
- W1 — Thinking Like a Model: identify the exact fact asked; answer vs. refuse.
- W2 — LLMs & Prompting: instruction style, concise phrasing, polite
  safe-refusals, citation wording.
- W3 — RAG (only): small-corpus retrieval with accurate citations from
  AMC/SEBI/AMFI pages.
